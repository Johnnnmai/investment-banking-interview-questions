<h2>What the interviewer is testing</h2><p>Whether you have done real research on the firm and can give reasons that would not apply equally to every other bank.</p>
<h2>Answer framework</h2><ol class=steps><li>Give two or three specific reasons, not generic praise.</li><li>Point to concrete strengths: a sector group, recent deal types, product strength or client base.</li><li>Use what you learned in conversations with people at the firm (without dropping names you cannot back up).</li><li>Link the firm&#x27;s structure, such as group size or generalist program, to how you want to learn.</li><li>Close by connecting it to your goals and the role you are interviewing for.</li></ol>
<h2>Sample answer</h2><div class=sample><p class=ans>There are three reasons. First, the firm&#x27;s position in industrials and business services fits my interest. In researching the group I read about several recent sell-side processes for mid-sized industrial companies, and that is exactly the kind of work I enjoyed when I built a comps analysis for a manufacturing target during my corporate development internship.</p><p class=ans>Second, the people I spoke with described a lean deal team structure, where analysts are close to senior bankers and clients. One associate told me she drafted sections of a management presentation in her first year and joined the client meetings to hear how it landed. I learn best when I can see how my work gets used, so that matters to me.</p><p class=ans>Third, the analyst program lets first-years work across products before specializing, which would let me build broad technical skills while staying in a sector I care about. Put together, it is the place where I think I would learn the most and contribute the fastest, and that is why I am excited about this role.</p></div>
<h2>Likely follow-ups</h2><div class=fu><b>Which other banks are you interviewing with?</b> Answer honestly and briefly, keep the focus on firms with similar strengths, and explain why this one is a strong fit.</div>
<h2>Common mistakes</h2><ul class=mist><li>Praising the bank&#x27;s reputation or culture in generic terms.</li><li>Giving reasons that apply to every bulge bracket or boutique.</li><li>Citing deals you cannot discuss in any detail.</li><li>Mentioning compensation or prestige as the main reason.</li></ul>
