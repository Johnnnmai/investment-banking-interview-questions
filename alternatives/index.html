What the paid guides do better

Be honest about the trade-off. The Red Book has ten times as many questions as our free bank. BIWS ships Excel case studies, which no PDF can replace if you face a modeling test. If either of those is your gap, buy that one.

Where the low-cost route wins

  1. You can check your level for free first. Read 50 deep dives and the full 100-question bank.
  2. You practice out loud, not just read. The free mock round timer draws random questions and the answer self-check scores what you said.
  3. You start with the 30 that come up most. The free IB Top 30 guide has model answers to the most-asked questions.

Which one should you pick?

  • Superday in a week, first time studying: the free Top 30 and the 100 free questions, then the Red Book if you still have time.
  • Modeling test or assessment center: BIWS for the case studies.
  • Industry group interview (FIG, TMT, restructuring): the Red Book’s industry sections.
  • No budget: the free bank, the mock round timer and the self-check.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to the Wall Street Prep Red Book? It depends on what you need. If you want more questions, the Red Book's 1,000 is hard to beat at $99. If you want Excel case studies, the BIWS IB Interview Guide ($197) includes 17. If you want to start free, the 100-question bank on this site and the free self-check cost nothing. The free IB Top 30 guide on Gumroad adds model answers to the 30 most-asked questions.
Is the BIWS IB Interview Guide worth $197? Its own page lists 578+ pages, 17 Excel-based case studies, 109+ fit questions with model answers and a 90-day money-back guarantee. If you have modeling tests or assessment centers coming, the case studies are the main reason to pay more.
Are free investment banking interview questions enough? For many first-round interviews, yes, if you practice out loud. Free sources include the 100 questions on this site and the widely shared 400 Questions guide from Breaking Into Wall Street. Paid guides add structure, depth and modeling practice.
How is the free IB Top 30 guide different from the free question bank? The bank gives 100 questions with answer frameworks. The Top 30 is a 13-page guide with model answers to the 30 most-asked questions, as a free download on Gumroad.
Do any of these replace mock interviews? No. Every option here is reading or self-practice. Pair any of them with mock interviews from your club or a mentor.
